Your week

    The activities

    Twelve blocks to build a week from

    • Sniff walk A slow lead walk where the nose sets the pace.
    • Free run Off-lead running where it is allowed and fenced or open.
    • Fetch & retrieve Throw, wait, retrieve to hand.
    • Training session Short reps of one skill, then stop while it is going well.
    • Nose game Hidden treats or a scent trail in the house or garden.
    • Food puzzle A meal from a puzzle feeder, snuffle mat or frozen toy.
    • Mat settle Quiet time on the mat while the house carries on.
    • Swim Water play where entry and exit are easy.
    • Play date Time with a dog friend your pointer already knows.
    • New place A new street, park or trail to look at and sniff.
    • Brushing A short brush and a look over coat, ears and paws.
    • Rest & chew A lazy block with a chew and nothing planned.

    Using the sheet

    Three tips for the printed week

    1. Plan, then adjust

      Sketch the week on Sunday and move blocks around when the weather or your diary changes.

    2. Tick what happened

      The printed sheet has a tick box in every slot, so everyone at home sees what is already done.

    3. Your numbers, or none

      The planner adds no times or distances. Type your own minutes for a slot, or leave them out.
