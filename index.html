05How it works

From blank week to fridge door

  1. Pick a start date

    The sheet runs for seven days from the date you choose.

  2. Paint the slots

    Choose an activity, then tap the morning, midday or evening slots it goes in.

  3. Check the mix

    A short summary counts each activity and the quiet blocks in your week.

  4. Print or save a PDF

    A4 or US Letter, landscape, with tick boxes to mark what you did.
